One window. Two panes.
Nothing to babysit.

Left is recordings, newest first. Right is the selected note. Models and Settings replace the right pane — no extra windows.

Record mic + computer audio

Records the selected microphone plus what your Mac is playing. If computer audio is not allowed, records mic only and says so honestly.

Meeting nudge, not auto-record

When Zoom, Meet, or Teams starts and you are not recording, Anchovy asks once via notification + banner: Record or Not now. No silent recording.

Live source + size

Always shows the mic name and whether computer audio is recording, with elapsed time and file size updating as you record.

On-device notes

Summary, Decisions, Action items, and Transcript in one note.md, beside audio.wav. Title stays English; body keeps the spoken language.

Your folder, your tools

Point at an existing Obsidian vault or a new folder. Any Markdown reader works. No Obsidian plugin in this version.

Retry without drama

Every recording has a status: Saved, Needs models, Working, Ready, Failed. Failed explains why — out of memory, disk full, download cut — with Retry. Audio is always kept.

First open takes three steps.

1

Pick a notes folder

Default ~/Documents/Anchovy, or choose your Obsidian vault.

2

Allow mic + computer audio

Each permission says what it is for. Without computer audio, the other side of online calls is not recorded — and the app says so.

3

Download default models

Shows total size and progress. Choose Later and still record; notes wait until models arrive.

You pick the model. The app does the rest.

A curated recommendation list ships with the app. Download, load, and unload are handled locally. New small models arrive with app updates.

Same rule in both packages: no account, no upload. This version does not encrypt notes — encryption for sharing comes later, in the style of Signal and WhatsApp.

What this version does not do.

  • No silent auto-recording of meetings
  • No live captions, mind maps, or Obsidian plugin
  • No speaker names stated as fact, no multi-track
  • No always-on ambient recording
  • No Intel Mac, iPhone, Windows, or recorder hardware in v1

Frequently asked questions

Which Macs are supported?

Apple Silicon Mac laptops. Intel Macs are not in v1. 16 GB is comfortable; 8 GB works because the two models never load at the same time.

Does it join my meetings?

No. It records the mic and computer audio on your Mac. It never joins Zoom, Meet, or Teams and never adds a bot.

What if it misses a meeting starting?

Browser tabs or renamed windows can fool meeting detection. Manual Record always works and records the same way.

Why is the first download so big?

Local models are gigabytes. The app shows total size and progress, verifies files, and lets you defer the download — recording still saves audio.

Where do my notes live?

In the folder you choose, one subfolder per recording: note.md + audio.wav (or audio.m4a on Small quality). Regenerate asks before overwriting edits you made in Obsidian.

How much does it cost?

Free. MIT licensed. Like Orca, the first app that solves the pain is open and free — no transcription fees, no subscription for this app.
